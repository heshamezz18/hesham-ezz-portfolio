ReceiptFlow AI

Problem: Manually reading receipt photos, sorting expenses into months, entering merchant names and totals, and building spreadsheet reports was slow and error-prone.

Solution: A secure English-language web app for bulk receipt upload, AI-assisted merchant/date/amount extraction, editable review, monthly and yearly filtering, private image storage, and Excel export.
